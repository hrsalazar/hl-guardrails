# Problem interviews: script (stage 1)

**Goal:** find out whether rule-break losses are a painful, recurring problem that traders already spend effort on. Whether they like the idea is not the question. See [product.md](../product.md), "Validation plan".

**Bar (fixed 2026-10-14):** at least 10 of 20 interviewees describe a rule-break loss in the last 3 months **and** have already tried something to fix it.
- **Below 6:** stop and keep the tool personal.
- **6–9:** run 10 more interviews with one change to who is interviewed, then judge all 30 together, at the same 50% bar.

## Who

- People who trade HL perps themselves, about weekly or more often. Not bots, vault managers or KOLs.
- **Where to find them:** HL community Discord and Telegram groups, crypto X replies, r/hyperliquid.
  - Ask in public threads, or ask people who reply to your posts.
  - **Never DM wallets found on-chain.**
- **Format:** a 20-minute call or a text chat. Offer the free wallet check as a thank-you.

## Rules (*The Mom Test*)

- Ask about their past, not their opinion of the future. "Would you use…" answers are worthless.
- Don't pitch until the last 2 minutes, if at all.
- Write down exact quotes, and anything they've **paid for or built** themselves.

## Questions

1. How do you trade on Hyperliquid? Account size range, how often, and which coins.
2. Tell me about your worst loss in the last 3 months. What happened, step by step?
3. Did you have a rule that would have prevented it (a stop, a size cap, no averaging down)? Why didn't it hold?
4. What did you do afterwards to stop it happening again?
5. What do you use today: a journal, alerts, a stop discipline, a bot, an app, a coach? What does it cost?
6. How often does something like that happen? What did the last one cost, roughly?
7. If something had warned you at that moment, what would it have needed to say for you to act on it?
8. *(Last, optional.)* Show the wallet check. "Would you want this live on your phone?" Then note whether they ask for a price or the waitlist, unprompted.
