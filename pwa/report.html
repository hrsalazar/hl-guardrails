How do your losses happen?

Most traders' losses don't come from trading often. They come from a few positions that were averaged down, held without a stop, or liquidated. See what your last 90 days on Hyperliquid look like, next to 145 other active traders.

Runs entirely in this browser. Your trades are read from Hyperliquid's public API (anyone can read them; no keys, no wallet connection) and nothing is stored or sent anywhere else.
Method: every closed perp position of the last 90 days, rebuilt from your fills (at most the latest 10,000) and order history (the latest 2,000 orders). Averaged down = added at a price at least 0.5% worse than your average entry. Stop = a reduce-only stop order placed between opening and closing (judged only where the order history reaches back that far). The comparison is an exploratory study of 145 random active wallets, 20,138 positions (study and caveats).
Information about your own past trades, not financial advice. It describes what happened; it doesn't predict what will.
